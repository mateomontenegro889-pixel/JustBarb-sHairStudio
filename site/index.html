The studio

The studio

Just Barb's is one chair, one stylist, and a room that smells like a proper salon should. You book with Barb and you see Barb — not whoever's free that day. There's a shelf of the products she actually trusts, Moroccanoil and Verb among them, and a corner with toys and books for the kids who come in with their parents, or on their own. It's the kind of place a neighbourhood in Haysboro is lucky to still have: unhurried, exact, and honest about what your hair actually needs.
